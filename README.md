## What I work on

### Business problems → AI solutions
I connect technical implementation with business needs: understanding existing workflows, identifying repetitive tasks, assessing where AI can help, and defining the inputs, outputs and human review steps. My work also includes testing, documentation and training to help teams use the resulting tools.

### Multimodal AI & assistant workflows
I develop workflows that combine **text, images, audio and documents**: collecting inputs, transcribing recordings, processing document content, generating structured responses and exporting usable outputs. Applications include reporting prototypes, meeting assistance, document synthesis and learning-content generation.

### Computer vision & recognition-model evaluation
My internship work on **license-plate recognition** focused on understanding model errors and improving data quality. I wrote Python scripts for character-level error analysis, examined recurring recognition mistakes, annotated images and prepared data to support subsequent retraining.

For document workflows, my OCR experience is primarily **integrating existing APIs**.

### Machine learning, deep learning & data analysis
My project experience includes **collaborative filtering for music recommendations**, recommendation evaluation, and financial data collection and preprocessing for a stock-index prediction project. My broader technical toolkit includes scikit-learn, PyTorch, TensorFlow and OpenCV, with familiarity in CNNs, RNNs and Transformers.

### Business workflow automation
I work on workflows for **information monitoring, meeting follow-up, CRM updates and content preparation**. This includes connecting APIs and business tools, collecting and filtering information, handling duplicates, structuring data, and introducing human validation before downstream actions.

### Application development & integration
I connect AI capabilities with APIs and web interfaces. My contributions include backend workflows, frontend integration, data validation, error handling and testing, alongside application features such as structured CSV imports and advertising-platform integration.

### AI education & knowledge sharing
I design and deliver practical learning experiences for **children, students and professionals**, covering AI, Python, programming and automation. I adapt explanations, exercises and demonstrations to the audience's technical background and goals.
